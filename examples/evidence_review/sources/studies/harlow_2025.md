# Harlow University (2025). "The four-day week in mixed workforces"

Working paper, pre-registered, not yet peer reviewed. Funded by a university grant; no conflicts declared.

## Abstract

We studied 12 firms that moved to a 32-hour week for 9 months, covering 1,560 employees. Overall output per employee did not change (+0.0%). Wellbeing improved: sick days fell 11% and voluntary attrition fell from 16% to 13%.

## Table 2. Output per employee by group

| Group | Employees | Measure | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge workers | 980 | Projects and deliverables completed | +5.2% |
| Customer-support shift staff | 580 | Tickets resolved per FTE | -8.7% |
| All employees | 1,560 | Weighted output index | +0.0% |

In the customer-support group, average customer wait time rose 31% and three of the five firms with support teams hired extra staff to restore coverage. Sick days fell in both groups.

## Limits

Nine months is short. Firms chose to take part, so they may differ from typical firms.
