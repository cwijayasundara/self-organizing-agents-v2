# Okafor, D. and Lind, S. (2024). "Compressed weeks, expanded output"

Published in the Journal of Workplace Studies, vol. 40.

## Design

Before-and-after comparison, no control group. 9 firms, 310 employees, observed for 4 months before and 4 months after a move to a 32-hour week.

## Results

| Measure | Change |
|---|---|
| Productivity (output per employee) | +22% |
| Employee satisfaction (1-5) | 3.4 to 4.3 |

The authors conclude that a four-day week "substantially raises productivity in every type of firm studied".

No funding statement or conflict-of-interest statement is given.
