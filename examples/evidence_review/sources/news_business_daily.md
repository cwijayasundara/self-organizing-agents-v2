# Business Daily, 14 January 2025: "Four-day week lifts productivity by 22%, landmark study finds"

A study in the Journal of Workplace Studies by Okafor and Lind has found that a four-day week raises productivity by 22%, a result the authors say holds "in every type of firm studied".

The finding adds to growing momentum. "Our clients see up to 40% higher productivity," said the chief executive of FlexWeek Solutions, a scheduling-software company. "The evidence is now overwhelming."

Not everyone is convinced. A labour economist interviewed for this article said that most trials so far have been in office jobs, and that "customer-facing teams with fixed opening hours are a different problem".
