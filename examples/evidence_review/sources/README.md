# Corvane Systems four-day week evidence room

Corvane Systems is a 600-person B2B software company. Leadership is deciding whether to move from a five-day to a four-day working week (32 hours, same pay).

Files in this evidence room:

- `README.md`: this index
- `studies/nordvik_2025.md`: field trial of a four-day week in office-based firms
- `studies/harlow_2025.md`: field trial of a four-day week in firms with mixed workforces
- `studies/okafor_lind_2024.md`: journal article on a four-day week and productivity
- `retraction_notices.md`: retraction notices published by the Journal of Workplace Studies
- `flexweek_whitepaper.md`: white paper on four-day week outcomes from a scheduling-software vendor
- `news_business_daily.md`: news article on four-day week research
- `staff_survey.csv`: Corvane internal staff survey on the four-day week, by department
- `cost_model.csv`: Corvane finance estimate of the annual cost of introducing a four-day week
