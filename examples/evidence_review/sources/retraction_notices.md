# Journal of Workplace Studies: retraction notices

## Notice 2025-03

The article Okafor, D. and Lind, S. (2024), "Compressed weeks, expanded output", Journal of Workplace Studies, vol. 40, is retracted at the request of the editors, March 2025. An independent check found that about a third of the survey responses were duplicated, and the authors could not supply the raw data. The reported productivity results cannot be relied on.

## Notice 2024-11

The article Petrov, A. (2023), "Open-plan offices and creative output", Journal of Workplace Studies, vol. 39, is retracted at the authors' request, November 2024, because of a coding error in the creativity scores.
